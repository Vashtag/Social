How to play

Goal: guess the hidden five-letter anatomy word in six tries.

How: type any real English word and press Enter — you don't have to guess anatomy words, only the answer is anatomical.

After each guess the tiles recolour:

Green — correct letter, correct spot.
Yellow — the letter is in the word, but a different spot.
Grey — the letter isn't in the word.

Solve it in as few guesses as you can. A new word every day, and the answer's definition appears when you finish.
